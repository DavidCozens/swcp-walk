From the ferry landing below East Portlemouth past Mill Bay and Gara Rock, out
round Prawle Point, the southernmost point of Devon, then by Lannacombe and
Start Point to the ruins of old Hallsands, Beesands and Torcross.

There is no bus along this coast. Getting back to Salcombe takes the 93S from
Torcross Stores inland to Kingsbridge, the 164 out to Salcombe and then the
ferry, about two hours: Mon–Sat the 14:08 from Torcross is the last clean chain,
reaching Salcombe at 15:03, and on Sundays the 14:20, in at 15:53. The ferry is
the binding leg — miss it and the road round is about 20 km. East Prawle,
Beesands and Hallsands have only the volunteer Coleridge Bus, one morning run a
week each, booked the evening before.
