Past Portholland to Porthluney Cove below Caerhays Castle, round Hemmick Beach
and out to Dodman Point. Then by Vault Beach to Gorran Haven, and on to
Portmellon and Mevagissey.

No bus runs between Portloe and Gorran Haven. Mon–Sat the way back is two
buses, changing at St Austell bus station: the 29 or 23 from Mevagissey, then
the 51 to Portloe; the last is the 29 from Mevagissey Trevarth at 16:56 for the
51 at 17:52, Portloe Church 18:32. There's no 51 on Sundays, so it's a taxi.
