Round the back of Cambeak, past The Strangles and over High Cliff, the highest
in Cornwall, then along Beeny Cliff and inland round the head of the Pentargon
valley, past its waterfall, down to Boscastle harbour. Out past the Willapark
lookout, along the cliffs by Trevalga and the Ladies Window, across Rocky
Valley and above Bossiney Haven to finish below Tintagel Castle.

The 95 calls at Crackington Haven, Boscastle, Bossiney and Tintagel about every
two hours, Sundays included through the summer, so the day splits at Boscastle.
Between Crackington and Boscastle the escapes are lanes, with no bus. Council
car parks at Boscastle and Tintagel ban motorhomes 11pm–8am.
