Out past Droskyn and the mine-scarred cliffs of Cligga Head, down into
Trevellas Porth and on to Trevaunance Cove below St Agnes. Round St Agnes
Head, past the Towanroath engine house at Wheal Coates to Chapel Porth, over
to Porthtowan, then along the fenced clifftop of RAF Portreath and down to
Portreath harbour.

No bus links the two ends. Back from Portreath, Mon–Sat, it's the 49 to
Redruth, the 315 to St Agnes and the 87 to Perranporth: four chains a day, the
last leaving Portreath at 16:54, with two-minute changes at St Agnes. Later,
and on Sundays, go via Truro. Dogs are banned from Porthtowan beach 15 May to
30 September and from Chapel Porth and Portreath beaches in July and August,
10am–6pm. The council car parks at St Agnes and Porthtowan ban vans overnight.
