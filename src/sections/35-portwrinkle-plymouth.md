Past Tregantle and through Freathy above Whitsand Bay, by Polhawn to Rame Head
and its chapel, round Penlee Point to Cawsand and Kingsand, then through Mount
Edgcumbe Country Park to Cremyll and the ferry to Plymouth.

The 70B links the two ends every day, from Cremyll to Crafthole, 850 m up the
lane from Portwrinkle: Mon–Sat at 06:45, 09:55 and 11:55, Sundays at 08:10 and
10:25. After the walk, Mon–Sat, the 70 from Cremyll with a change at Antony to
the 70B or the 75 reaches Crafthole by 20:46 at the latest; on Sundays only the
16:25 connects. The 70 group also calls at Tregantle, Freathy, Polhawn and
Cawsand, but Military Road is closed to buses until 2 October 2026, so until then
nothing calls at Freathy, Polhawn or Rame. From April to October the Cawsand
Ferry runs from Cawsand beach to Plymouth's Barbican.
