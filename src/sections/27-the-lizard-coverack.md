Round Housel Bay past the Lizard Wireless Station at Bass Point, down to Church
Cove, then by the Devil's Frying Pan into the fishing village of Cadgwith. On
to Kennack Sands, then a long, empty stretch over Carrick Luz and Black Head to
Chynhalls Point and Coverack.

Coverack has no Sunday bus. Mon–Sat, back to the Lizard is the 36 to Helston
Sainsbury's and the 34 to Lizard village, an hour and a half to two and a half
hours; the first leaves Coverack at 07:20 and the last at 18:57. The 34 also
calls at Ruan Minor and Kuggar, up the lanes from Cadgwith and Kennack Sands.
Dogs are banned from Housel Bay and Kennack Sands beaches in July and August,
10am–6pm. The car park at the entrance to Coverack is signed no overnight
camping.
