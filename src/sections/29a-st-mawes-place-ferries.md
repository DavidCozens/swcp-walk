Two ferries carry the path from Falmouth to the Roseland. The St Mawes Ferry
crosses Carrick Roads from Falmouth's Prince of Wales Pier to St Mawes all year,
up to three an hour in summer and hourly in winter, in about 20 minutes; from
April to October some sailings also use Custom House Quay. From St Mawes the
Place ferry crosses the Percuil river to Place, April to October only, 9am to
5pm from St Mawes, up to every half hour, subject to tide and weather; it takes
about 10 minutes and dogs on leads are welcome. Out of season there is no way
across to Place but a long way round by road.
