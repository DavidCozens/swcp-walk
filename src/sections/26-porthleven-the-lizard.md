Along the cliffs from Porthleven to Loe Bar, the shingle bank holding back Loe
Pool, past the Anson memorial to Gunwalloe Fishing Cove and Church Cove. Over
Poldhu by the Marconi monument to Mullion Cove's harbour, then the long
clifftop over Predannack to Kynance Cove, and on to Lizard Point, the southern
tip of mainland Britain, and the lighthouse.

Never swim at Loe Bar: the shingle shelves steeply and the undertow is fierce
even on calm days. The 34 is the only bus: Lizard village to Helston hourly
Mon–Sat and every two hours on Sundays, by Mullion and Poldhu; change at
Helston for the 2 to Porthleven. Dogs are banned from Poldhu and Kynance Cove
beaches in July and August, 10am–6pm. The National Trust car parks, Kynance
and Lizard Point included, don't allow vans overnight, and Poldhu's bans them
11.30pm–8am.
