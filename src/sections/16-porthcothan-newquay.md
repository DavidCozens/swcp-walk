Round the coves by Porth Mear to Park Head, then along the clifftop above the
stacks of Bedruthan Steps to Carnewas. Down to Mawgan Porth, over to the long
beach at Watergate Bay, and on along the cliffs by Porth and Barrowfields into
Newquay.

The staircase down to Bedruthan beach has been closed since a rock fall in
2019. The 56 back to Porthcothan leaves Newquay bus station about hourly, the
last at 17:15 (17:20 on Sundays), and goes inland, so there's no bus at
Carnewas. Dogs are banned from Porth beach 1 July to 31 August, 10am–6pm;
Mawgan Porth and Watergate Bay take them all year. Watergate Bay car park is
the one council car park round Newquay where a self-contained van may stay
overnight; those in the town ban vans 11pm–8am.
