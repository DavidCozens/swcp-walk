Out of Padstow along the estuary to Hawker's Cove and round Stepper Point and
its daymark, past the collapsed caves at Pepper Hole and Trevone's Round Hole
to Trevone and Harlyn Bay. Round Mother Ivey's Bay by the lifeboat station to
Trevose Head, then Constantine Bay, Treyarnon's rock pool and a run of coves to
Porthcothan.

The 56 runs between Porthcothan and Padstow all year, hourly on weekdays and
about every two hours at weekends, calling near Trevone, at Harlyn Bridge and
at Constantine Bay. Dogs are banned from Trevone beach 15 May to 30 September,
10am–6pm, and from St George's Well at Padstow in July and August. No car
park on this stretch allows vans overnight.
