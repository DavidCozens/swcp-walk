Out past Porthmeor beach and Clodgy Point onto the wildest stretch of the path,
over 1,000 m of climbing with no village on it. Past Zennor Head and Pendour
Cove, Gurnard's Head, and Bosigran's climbing cliffs below the Carn Galver
engine houses, then round Portheras Cove to Pendeen lighthouse.

The coast bus, the 7, runs between St Ives and Pendeen only in the summer
holidays, 26 July to 31 August 2026, four a day. Otherwise, Mon–Sat, it's the
4 from Pendeen village to Penzance and the 17 to St Ives, the last connection
leaving Pendeen at 17:18; the 16A calls at Zennor and Gurnard's Head. On
Sundays the connection at Penzance misses by minutes. Dogs are banned from
Porthmeor beach 15 May to 30 September, 10am–6pm. No car park on this stretch
allows vans overnight; the Old Coastguards campsite is beside the lighthouse.
