Past Talland Bay and round Hannafore Point into West Looe, over the bridge and
through East Looe, then by Millendreath, Seaton and Downderry to Portwrinkle.

No Sunday buses run along this coast east of Looe. Mon–Sat the way back is the
75 from Portwrinkle Finnygook Beach, changing at Hessenford to the 10 or at
Liskeard to the 73: the first leaves at 09:12, Polperro Crumplehorn 11:10; the
last at 15:39 on schooldays, or 16:12 on Saturdays and in school holidays. The
10 and 73 run between Looe and Polperro every day, and Looe's trains go to
Liskeard.
