Along the canal to the sea lock and up past the Storm Tower on Compass Point,
then over Efford Down and past Upton to Widemouth Bay. South of Widemouth the
path follows the cliff road over Penhalt Cliff and down through Millook, where
the cliffs are folded into zigzags. On past the dwarf oak wood at Dizzard Point
and round Pencannow Point to Crackington Haven.

The 95 calls at Widemouth Bay and Crackington Haven, six a day Mon–Sat all
year, so the day splits at Widemouth. Dogs are banned from Widemouth and
Crackington Haven beaches from 15 May to 30 September, 10am–6pm. Every council
car park at Widemouth bans motorhomes 11pm–8am.
