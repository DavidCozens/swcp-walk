Over Mayon Cliff by Maen Castle to Land's End, then south by Nanjizal and the
climbing cliffs of Chair Ladder to Gwennap Head and Porthgwarra. On past St
Levan to the Minack Theatre above Porthcurno, round the Logan Rock headland to
Penberth Cove, and by St Loy and Tater Du lighthouse to Lamorna Cove.

Back from Lamorna, walk 1.6 km up the valley road to Lamorna Turn for the 1 to
Sennen Cove, 27 minutes: Mon–Sat at 08:07, then about every two hours to
17:59. The 1A fills the other hours by Porthcurno to Land's End, where the 1
carries on to the cove. Dogs are banned from Sennen beach 15 May to 30
September and from Porthcurno beach in July and August, 10am–6pm. No car park
on this stretch allows vans overnight: Land's End sells vans a day ticket only.
