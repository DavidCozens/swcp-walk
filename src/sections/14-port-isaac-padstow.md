Out to Port Quin and Doyden Castle, on by Lundy Bay and round the headland by
The Rumps and Pentire Point, where Binyon wrote "For the Fallen". Down to
Polzeath, round to Daymer Bay, past St Enodoc church below Brea Hill and along
the estuary to Rock, then the ferry across the Camel to Padstow.

The Black Tor Ferry runs daily all year from 8am; the last boat from Rock is
4.45pm in winter and 7.30pm in August, and a water taxi covers the evenings
until 11pm, April to October. Without either it's the 96 to Wadebridge and the
57 to Padstow, Mon–Sat. Dogs are banned from Polzeath beach 15 May to 30
September, 10am–6pm. Link Road car park is the one place in Padstow a van may
stay overnight.
