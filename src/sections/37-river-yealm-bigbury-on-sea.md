From the ferry landing below Noss Mayo along the old carriage drive past the
watch tower and the ruined church at Stoke Point, then over Beacon Hill to
Mothecombe. Across the Erme at Wonwell, on by Westcombe and Ayrmer Cove to
Challaborough and Bigbury-on-Sea.

There is no ferry over the Erme and never has been one: the river is too
shallow, and South Devon National Landscape, which runs the estuary
conservation group, says the crossing is made by wading between the slipways at
Mothecombe and Wonwell for an hour either side of low tide. Outside that window
it is too deep, and heavy rain upstream or a swell at the mouth can close it
altogether. There is no path round: the land is a private estate, and the lanes
by Sequer's Bridge, the lowest bridge, are about 13 km. The tide table decides
what time this day starts.

No bus reaches Bigbury-on-Sea, Challaborough, Kingston or Wonwell. Devon's Fare
Car — the F17 to Kingsbridge, the F7 to Modbury and Ivybridge — is a shared car
on a timetable, booked the day before on 0345 155 1015. The 94 calls at
Battisborough Cross, 1.4 km up the lane from Mothecombe, twice a day Mon–Sat.
Getting back to the Yealm is three legs and nothing on a Sunday, so in practice
it is a taxi.
