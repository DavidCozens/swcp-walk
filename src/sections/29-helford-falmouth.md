Along the Helford past Trebah's beach and Durgan, below Glendurgan Garden, and
out to Mawnan church at the mouth of the river. Round Rosemullion Head to
Maenporth, then by Swanpool and Gyllyngvase beaches into Falmouth, round
Pendennis Point below the castle to the docks and the town.

The way back is the 35 or 63 from Falmouth The Moor to the top of the lane at
Helford Passage, about hourly Mon–Sat, 20–25 minutes, 07:10–17:45. No Sunday
buses. The 67 and 64A run from Swanpool and Pendennis Point into Falmouth.
