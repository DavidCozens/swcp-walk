A passenger ferry across the Helford river from the jetty at Helford Point,
below Helford village, to Helford Passage by the Ferry Boat Inn. It runs on
demand, allowing for the tide, 9.30am–5pm every day from 1 April to 31 October,
and to 6pm from 7 July to 7 September. From the village side, open the yellow
circular sign so the ferry can see you waiting. Dogs travel free. Out of season
it's a taxi round, most of a day by bus Mon–Fri, or about 21 km on roads by
Gweek.
