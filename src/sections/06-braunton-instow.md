Level all day, on the Tarka Trail, shared with cyclists. Out of Braunton on the
old Ilfracombe railway, alongside the airfield fence at RMB Chivenor and the
north bank of the Taw into Barnstaple. Over the medieval Long Bridge, then back
west on the old railway to Bideford along the south bank, past Fremington Quay
and Isley Marsh, to the quay at Instow.
