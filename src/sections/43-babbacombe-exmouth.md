Over Petit Tor and down through Watcombe and Maidencombe, along the red cliffs
past Labrador Bay to Shaldon, then over Shaldon Bridge into Teignmouth. North on
the sea wall by Holcombe into Dawlish, out along the wall again to Dawlish
Warren, and round the estuary by Cockwood to the ferry pier at Starcross.

The 11 is the bus for this section: St Marychurch to Dawlish Warren, hourly every
day, and the only one at Maidencombe, Labrador Bay and Shaldon. It stops 4 km
short of Starcross, so coming back from the pier means the train — Starcross to
Torquay in about 33 minutes, then the 22 up to St Marychurch. When storms shut
the sea wall line, which they do, the 2 bus between Exeter, Dawlish and
Teignmouth takes rail tickets.
