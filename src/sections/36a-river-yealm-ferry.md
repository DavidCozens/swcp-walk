A passenger ferry across the mouth of the Yealm from Warren Point, on the
Wembury side, to Wide Slip on Passage Road below Noss Mayo; ask and it will land
you at Yealm Steps for Newton Ferrers instead. It is an electric launch that
comes when called rather than a boat on a timetable: ring or text 07399 067942,
or call on VHF channel 69. It runs every day from 28 March to 30 September, 10am
to 4pm, and from 10 July to 30 September 9am to 5pm Sunday to Thursday and 9am to
9pm on Friday and Saturday. Shore to shore is £5, £2.50 for a child aged 5 to 14,
and the price list says nothing about dogs. Weather, sea state and tide cut the
hours back, so ring before relying on it. Out of season there is no crossing at
all: a taxi round by Brixton and Yealmpton, or Monday to Saturday the 48 bus from
Wembury and the 94 on from Elburton.
