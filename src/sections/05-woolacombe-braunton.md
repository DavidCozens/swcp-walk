Behind Woolacombe's dunes on Marine Drive to Putsborough, then round Baggy
Point to Croyde Bay. The route touches Croyde beach, which bans dogs from 1 May
to 30 September. Along the cliffs above the coast road to Saunton, then south
down the landward side of Braunton Burrows and along the American Road, laid by
US troops training for D-Day, to the estuary at Crow Point. The last 4 km
follow the Taw and the River Caen to Velator Quay.
