Along Exmouth seafront to Orcombe Point and the Geoneedle, where the Jurassic
Coast begins, over Sandy Bay and Straight Point to Budleigh Salterton. Up the
Otter to the footbridge at South Farm and back down the far bank, then the red
cliffs by Ladram Bay, High Peak and Peak Hill into Sidmouth.

The Otter has no crossing at its mouth: the path turns inland for about a
kilometre each way to the footbridge. East Devon allows overnight motorhome
parking at two car parks in Exmouth, Maer Road and the Queen's Drive echelon,
and nowhere else along this coast.
