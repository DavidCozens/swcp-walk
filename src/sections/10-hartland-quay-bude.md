Past St Catherine's Tor and the waterfall at Speke's Mill Mouth, along the
cliffs by Embury Beacon to Welcombe Mouth, and over Marsland Mouth into
Cornwall. On by Henna Cliff to Morwenstow and Hawker's Hut, past the GCHQ
dishes above Lower Sharpnose Point, then Duckpool, Sandymouth and Northcott
Mouth. Into Bude past Crooklets and the sea pool, crossing the Neet by Nanny
Moore's Bridge.

No shop on the way. The only bus near the path is the weekday 217, from Morwenstow and Coombe to Bude. Dogs are banned from Crooklets beach from
15 May to 30 September, 10am–6pm, and must be on a lead on Summerleaze from
21 May to 30 September, 10am–6pm.
