Almost level, and beside the water most of the day. The Tarka Trail up the
east bank of the Torridge, under the A39 bridge, to East-the-Water; over the
medieval Long Bridge into Bideford and north along the Quay. Riverside paths
and lanes below Northam to Appledore's quay and lifeboat station, then round the
Skern saltmarsh and the edge of Northam Burrows to the tip of the estuary, and
back along the road behind the Pebble Ridge into Westward Ho!.

From 1 May to 30 September dogs are banned from Westward Ho! beach south of
Sandymere, and must be on a lead on the promenade.
