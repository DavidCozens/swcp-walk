Along the seafront out of Westward Ho! and over Kipling Tors, then cliffs
past Cornborough and Abbotsham. From Peppercombe, with its red cliffs, most of
the way is in woodland: down to Bucks Mills and out again, then the last 4 km
on the Hobby Drive, a carriage road cut through the cliff woods between 1811
and 1829, to the top of Clovelly.
