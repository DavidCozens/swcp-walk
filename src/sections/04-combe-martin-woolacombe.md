Round Watermouth Cove and over the headlands to Hele Bay, then past
Hillsborough into Ilfracombe's harbour and out along the Torrs. Inland by lane
and field to Lee, then back to the cliffs past Bull Point and round Morte Point
to Woolacombe's sands.
