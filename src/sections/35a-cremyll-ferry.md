A passenger ferry across the mouth of the Tamar from Cremyll Quay, below
Mount Edgcumbe, to Admiral's Hard in Stonehouse, Plymouth. It runs all year,
about every 30 minutes, and takes about 8 minutes: from Cremyll on the hour and half
past, from Admiral's Hard at quarter past and quarter to. The first leaves
Cremyll at 6.45am on weekdays, 7.30am on Saturdays and 8.30am on Sundays; the
last between 9pm and 10pm from April to September, and between 6pm and 7pm from
October to March. Well-behaved dogs travel free. From Admiral's Hard the 34 bus
goes into the city centre. When the ferry isn't running, the 70 bus from Cremyll
goes round by Millbrook and the Torpoint chain ferry, well over an hour.
