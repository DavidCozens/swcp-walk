Along the North Cliffs past Basset's Cove to Hell's Mouth, round Navax Point
and Godrevy Point, looking out to the lighthouse and down on the seals at
Mutton Cove, then onto the sand and dunes of Gwithian, Upton and Hayle Towans
to the mouth of the Hayle and round into the town.

No bus runs along the B3301. Back from Hayle, Mon–Sat, the 14 or 18 to Pool
and the 49 to Portreath, hourly, the last arriving at 18:54; evenings and
Sundays the 48 from Pool. The 14 also calls in Gwithian village, the one bus
near the path. Dogs are banned from the beaches between Godrevy Point and
Harvey's Towans 1 July to 31 August, 10am–6pm, and from the Hayle estuary,
Carnsew Pool and Copperhouse Pool all year. Gwithian Towans car park bans vans
11pm–8am.
