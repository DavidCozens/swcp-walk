Through Hayle past Harvey's Foundry and along the estuary to Griggs Quay,
over the river by the RSPB reserve and along Lelant Saltings to St Uny's
church. Through the dunes above Porth Kidney Sands to Carbis Bay, then round
Porthminster Point into St Ives.

The 14 runs between Hayle and St Ives hourly every day, about 20 minutes. The
St Ives Bay Line runs half-hourly from Carbis Bay and St Ives to St Erth;
Lelant is a request stop, and Lelant Saltings sees only one or two trains a
day. Dogs are banned from the Hayle estuary all year, and from Carbis Bay and
Porthminster beaches 15 May to 30 September, 10am–6pm. Trenwith car park is
the one council car park in St Ives where a self-contained van may stay
overnight.
