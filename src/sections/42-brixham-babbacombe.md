Out of Brixham by Fishcombe and Churston Coves to Elberry and Broadsands, then
the seafronts of Goodrington, Paignton, Preston and Livermead into Torquay. Round
the harbour and out past London Bridge to Meadfoot, Hope's Nose and Anstey's
Cove, over Walls Hill to Babbacombe Downs and the St Marychurch road.

Torbay's buses are frequent but none runs the whole way: back to Brixham means
the 22 or the 11 into Torquay's Cary Parade, then the 12 to Brixham, which goes
every 12 to 15 minutes Mon–Sat and through the night at weekends. The 120 takes
the coast road from Paignton to Churston and Kingswear, hourly. Torbay Council
allows no overnight sleeping in any of its car parks.
