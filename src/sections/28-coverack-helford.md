Out to Lowland Point, then past the quarries to Porthoustock and on to
Porthallow. Round Nare Point to Gillan Creek, across to St Anthony-in-Meneage,
and round Dennis Head up the Helford to Helford village and the ferry.

Gillan Creek is crossed on stepping stones for a few hours either side of low
water, or April to October by Sailaway's ferry on demand, £5, dogs free.
Otherwise it's about 3.5 km round by the head of the creek. No bus links the two
ends, and none runs at weekends. Mon–Fri the 36B leaves Helford Car Park at 14:03
for St Keverne, 14:28, where the 36 goes on to Coverack at 14:50 in school
holidays only; on schooldays walk the 3.3 km from St Keverne or take a taxi.
