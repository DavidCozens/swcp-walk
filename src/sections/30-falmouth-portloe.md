Past St Anthony's church and along the shore of Carrick Roads, by Great and
Little Molunan, to the lighthouse below St Anthony Head. Then north up the
Roseland past Porthbeor and Towan beaches to Portscatho, on by Porthcurnick,
Pendower and Carne beaches, and round Nare Head to Portloe.

No bus links the two ends, and nothing reaches St Anthony Head. Mon–Sat, April
to October, the way back is the 51 from Portloe Church to Ruan High Lanes, the 50
to St Mawes and the Place ferry: the last that makes it leaves Portloe at 13:13,
St Mawes 14:17. On Sundays, and November to March when the Place ferry doesn't
run, it's a taxi. The 50 calls at Portscatho and Pendower Turn every day.
