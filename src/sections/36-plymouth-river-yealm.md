From Admiral's Hard along the Hoe, past Smeaton's Tower, to the Barbican, then
round the Cattewater by Laira Bridge, Hooe and Turnchapel to Mount Batten. Out
past Jennycliff and Bovisand to Heybrook Bay, then over Wembury Point to Wembury
beach and the church, and along the Yealm to Warren Point.

The Mount Batten ferry cuts out the long way round the Cattewater: every 30
minutes all year, about 4 minutes, £3.25, dogs free. Back from Wembury, Mon–Sat,
the 48 from Southland Park, about 500 m up the lane from the beach car park,
reaches Royal Parade in 38 minutes, and the 34 goes on to Admiral's Hard; the
last leaves Southland Park at 18:47, and on weekdays there's nothing between
14:40 and 17:00. No Sunday buses at Wembury, Bovisand or Heybrook Bay.
