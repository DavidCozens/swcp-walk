Out of Mevagissey over Polstreath to Pentewan, round Black Head and past
Ropehaven Cliffs to Porthpean, then by Charlestown's harbour and past Carlyon
Bay into Par.

Buses go back to Mevagissey every day, changing at St Austell. Mon–Sat the 24
leaves Par opp Costcutter at 43 past the hour, for the 29 at 35 past; the last is
the 19:43, Mevagissey 20:55. On Sundays the 24A leaves Par at 09:47, 11:47, 13:47
and 15:47. From May to early October the Mevagissey ferry sails from Fowey: the
24 from Par Moorland Road at 16:08 makes the 16:50 ferry, Mevagissey 17:30. Dogs
aren't allowed on Pentewan's beach in summer.
