Up past St Materiana's onto Glebe Cliff and along the old slate quarries,
past the pinnacle at Lanterdan, down to Trebarwith Strand. On above Backways
Cove and the wartime bombing range to Tregardock, then nearly 7 km of cliffs
with no way off to Port Gaverne, and over the hill into Port Isaac's harbour.

Mon–Sat there's no direct bus between the ends: the 95 from Tintagel meets the
96 to Port Isaac at Delabole or Wadebridge. On Sundays in season the 95 runs
through in 48 minutes. There's no legal overnight van parking in Port Isaac,
and the council car park at Trebarwith Strand bans motorhomes 11pm–8am.
