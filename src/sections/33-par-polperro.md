Round Par Sands to Polkerris, out to the daymark on Gribbin Head, then past St
Catherine's Castle and Readymoney Cove into Fowey. The Polruan ferry crosses the
river, and the path climbs past Lantic Bay and Pencarrow Head, below Lansallos,
to Polperro.

The Polruan ferry runs every day but Christmas Day, every 10–15 minutes, from
7am until 7pm in winter and later in summer; £3.50, dogs 50p. It uses
Whitehouse Slip only on summer days, 16 May to 15 September, and Fowey's Town
Quay otherwise. Back to Par every day: the 73 from Polperro Crumplehorn to
Liskeard, then the train; the last Mon–Fri is the 17:09, Par 18:38, and on
Sundays they run every two hours, 08:50–16:50. Mon–Fri the 481 from Polperro to
Polruan also connects with the ferry and the 24 from Fowey.
