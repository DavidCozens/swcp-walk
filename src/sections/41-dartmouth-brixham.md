From the Lower Ferry slip up through Kingswear and out past Kingswear Castle to
Froward Point, through the wartime batteries at Brownstone and on below Coleton
Fishacre. Then the empty middle: Scabbacombe Sands, Man Sands and the cliffs to
Sharkham Point, St Mary's Bay and Berry Head, and round the headland into
Brixham.

The Lower Ferry runs all day, every day, and from Kingswear until 10.45pm, so
the two ends are easy to join. The 18 links them directly, Brixham Town Square
to Kingswear Banjo in 16 minutes, hourly every day: last out of Brixham 18:30
Mon–Sat and 17:36 on Sundays. Nothing at all runs between Kingswear and
Brixham's edge, and the middle of the section is a long way from a road. Brixham
has a community hospital but no minor injuries unit: Torbay Hospital is the one
to head for.
