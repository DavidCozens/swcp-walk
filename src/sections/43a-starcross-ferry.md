A passenger ferry across the mouth of the Exe from Starcross Pier, beside the
station, to the ferry steps in Exmouth Marina. It runs from April to the end of
October, seven days a week including bank holidays, and takes about twenty
minutes: from Starcross on the hour and ten minutes past, 10.10am to 4.10pm,
and from Exmouth at twenty to, 10.40am to 4.40pm, with a 5.10pm and a 5.40pm
added from 1 June to 31 August. Be at the pier ten minutes before: no place can
be booked and at peak times the boat fills. The pier is reached by steps over
the railway bridge, so it is no good with wheels. Adult £6 single and £10
return, children 1–12 £4 and £5, dogs £1, bikes £2 — cash only, on board. Tides
and weather stop it, and the day's times go on the operator's Facebook page
rather than the website, so check before walking down. Out of season, and on a
blown-out day, it is the train: Starcross to Exeter St Davids, then the Avocet
Line from Exeter Central to Exmouth, about an hour and a quarter for what the
ferry does in twenty minutes. There is no other way over: the far bank is two
kilometres across the water and twenty-five round by Exeter.
