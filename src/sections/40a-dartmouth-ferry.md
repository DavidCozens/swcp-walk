A vehicle ferry across the Dart from the Lower Ferry slip in Dartmouth to the
slip at Kingswear, where section 41 picks the path up. It is a tug pushing a
float, the last of its kind in England, and it runs continuously rather than to
a timetable, all year but Christmas Day: Mon–Sat from Dartmouth 7.10am to
10.55pm, Sundays from 8.10am, and from Kingswear ten minutes earlier either
way. Adult single £2.50 on foot, day return £5; under-16s £1. Cards, though not
American Express. Wheelchair accessible. Two floats work it normally and one
during maintenance, so check the operator's live status or ring 01803 752342.
If it stops, the passenger ferry lands at the same Kingswear pontoon and the
Higher Ferry crosses a kilometre upstream.
