Up the Avon to Cockleridge and over by the Bantham ferry, then round Thurlestone
and South Milton Sands to Hope Cove. Over Bolt Tail and along Bolberry Down and
the cliffs past Soar Mill Cove to Bolt Head, then down to South Sands, North
Sands and Salcombe.

The Bantham ferry runs 1 April to 30 September, 10am to noon and 2pm to 4pm
daily whatever the tide, £4.95 each way. Out of season the way round is the Avon
Estuary Walk by Aveton Gifford, about 12 km, or 14 at high tide when the tidal
road floods. No one bus links the two ends. Back to Bigbury, any day: the 164
from Salcombe to Kingsbridge, about 20 minutes, then the 3 to Aveton Gifford
Bridge End, about 20 more, and the Avon Estuary Walk down the west bank,
roughly 6 km. The last Sunday chain leaves Salcombe at 16:30. The 162 to
Thurlestone and Hope Cove runs Mon–Fri only.
