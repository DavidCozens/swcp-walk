A passenger ferry across the Salcombe estuary from the ferry steps at the
bottom of Fore Street, by the Ferry Inn, to the slipway below East Portlemouth.
It runs every day of the year, every half hour, and takes about five minutes:
from Salcombe on the hour and half past, from East Portlemouth five minutes
later. The national timetable data has the first at 8am and the last at 5.30pm
from Salcombe; the operator's own listing gives 8.30am to 5pm in winter and
8.30am to 6.30pm in summer, so check late in the day. From 1 April to 31
October it leaves the Jubilee Pier below the ferry steps, and from 1 November
to 31 March the Whitestrand pontoon in the middle of town, 300 m north. If
nobody is aboard, stand on the landing where the ferryman can see you. Dogs are
carried; the fare is about £2.50, cash or card, though the operator publishes
none. There is no other way over: the far bank is 400 m across the water and
about 20 km round by Kingsbridge.
