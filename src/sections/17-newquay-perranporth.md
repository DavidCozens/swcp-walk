Round Towan Head by the Huer's Hut to Fistral and out round Pentire Point East,
then up the north bank of the Gannel to the bridleway bridge and back down the
far side to Crantock. Over West Pentire to Porth Joke and Kelsey Head, down to
Holywell, past Penhale Camp and along the dunes above Perran Beach to
Perranporth.

The Gannel decides the day. Going round by the bridge adds about 5 km to the
SWCPA's 20.2. The Penpol footbridge is passable 3–4
hours either side of low water, and the Fern Pit ferry runs 10am–6pm from mid
May until the tides change in mid September. The path by North Pier in Newquay
has been closed and diverted since a landslip in February 2026. A rock fall
sealed the holy well cave at Holywell on 30 August 2026. Across Penhale the
path is the only public access through the MOD training area. The 87 runs
from Perranporth back to Newquay about hourly, every two hours on Sundays.
Dogs are welcome on Crantock beach all year. Wheal Leisure car park in
Perranporth allows self-contained vans overnight, with a 3,050 kg limit.
