Up to Lynton and out along the Valley of Rocks, then woods and high cliff
paths past Woody Bay and round the Heddon valley. The last stretch crosses open
moorland to Great Hangman, the highest point on the whole path, before dropping
steeply into Combe Martin. The longest and hardest of the first three days.
