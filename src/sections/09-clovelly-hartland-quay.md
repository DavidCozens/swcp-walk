Through the Clovelly Court woods, past Angel's Wings and Gallantry Bower, down
to Mouth Mill and up over Windbury Head. Then open cliff-top above farmland,
past Exmansworthy and Shipload Bay, to Hartland Point, where the coast turns
south. The rest runs past Blackpool Mill and the Warren to Hartland Quay.

No sleeping in vehicles in any of the three Hartland Quay car parks, which
charge £4 a day from Easter to October half term. Dogs are banned from the
beach there from 1 April to 31 October.
