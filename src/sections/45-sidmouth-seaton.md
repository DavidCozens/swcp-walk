The steep one: up Salcombe Hill out of Sidmouth, over Dunscombe and down to
Weston Mouth, then Branscombe Mouth, the Hooken landslip and Beer Head into Beer
and along to Seaton — 753 m of climbing in 15.6 km.

There is nothing between Sidmouth and Branscombe Mouth, nearly 10 km of cliff
and combe with no road down but the lanes at Weston and Salcombe Regis. The Sea
Shanty on the beach at Branscombe is the only food before Beer.
