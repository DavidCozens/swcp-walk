Along the Tin Coast past Geevor, the Levant beam engine and the engine houses
of Botallack on the rocks, down into the Kenidjack valley and round Cape
Cornwall. Past Ballowall Barrow to Porth Nanven's boulder beach at the foot of
the Cot Valley, then over the cliffs to Gwynver and along Whitesand Bay to
Sennen Cove.

The coast bus, the 7, joins Pendeen and Sennen Cove by St Just only in the
summer holidays, 26 July to 31 August 2026. Otherwise, back from Sennen Cove,
Mon–Sat, the 1 to Penzance and the 4 to Pendeen, about every two hours, the
last arriving at 20:19; on Sundays three a day. The 4 also runs hourly between
Pendeen, Botallack and St Just. Dogs are banned from Priest's Cove below Cape
Cornwall from Easter to 30 September, and from Sennen beach 15 May to 30
September, 10am–6pm. No car park on this stretch allows vans overnight.
