Along the shingle bar past Slapton Ley to Strete Gate, up to Strete and down to
Blackpool Sands, then by Stoke Fleming and Little Dartmouth to Dartmouth Castle
and into the town.

Storm Ingrid destroyed the A379 along Slapton Sands in January 2026 and it was
still closed in September, between Torcross and Strete Gate. The buses were
rebuilt around it: the 3 from Kingsbridge to Dartmouth now runs inland by
Halwell and Blackawton, calling at Strete, Blackpool Sands and Stoke Fleming,
and the 93S shuttle is the only bus at Torcross. Back from Dartmouth, Mon–Fri,
the 3 at 15:30 and the 93S at Kingsbridge make the last connection to Torcross,
17:06; on Sundays the 14:55 gets in at 16:49. Better Mon–Sat: the 93T from
Dartmouth Pontoon at 10:00, 12:00, 14:00 or 16:30 reaches Slapton Turn in 28
minutes, and from there it's 2.5 km back along the bar, on the path itself.
